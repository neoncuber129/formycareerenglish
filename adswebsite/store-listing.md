## Full description (adapt length per store)

**English**

FormyCareer helps professionals build vocabulary that sticks—through spaced repetition (SRS), tagging, and a personal library tuned for work and study.

**Desktop highlights**

- **Auto capture**: Track mouse text selections in other apps (when enabled) and open a lookup popup after a short pause.
- **Shortcuts**: Capture from selection or trigger region OCR with configurable hotkeys.
- **Lookup popup**: Translate, refine selection in the source text, hear pronunciation where available, and save cards fast.
- **Visual guides**: Step-by-step help for capture flows built into the app.

**Study**

- SRS queue with due tracking on the dashboard.
- Mixed review modes on **Pro** (forward, reverse meaning, reverse audio combined).

**Plans**

- **Free**: Fair daily caps on reviews, saves, and popup translations (loaded from server config); ads may appear in capture contexts when enabled.
- **Pro**: Removes desktop banner ads and lifts daily caps per current policy—activate via license key from purchase email.

**Privacy & terms**

Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are published at the URLs above. Capture features access text or screen regions you explicitly choose; use only content you’re allowed to process.
