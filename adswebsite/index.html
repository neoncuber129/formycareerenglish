What FormyCareer does

FormyCareer is a vocabulary-first learning app. You collect words and phrases that matter to your real life - technical terms, workplace English, interview language, or any pair you study - and the app helps you retain them with spaced repetition.

The product is built around retrieval practice: you are prompted to recall meaning on a schedule that strengthens memory over time.

  • Create and manage personal vocabulary with source text and translation.
  • Organize entries with tags, filters, search, and archive.
  • Run SRS-based review sessions with multiple prompt modes.

FAQ

Is FormyCareer a full language course?

No. It is a vocabulary and retention system focused on practical words and phrases.

What are the core implemented features today?

Vocabulary management, tag/search/archive organization, SRS review, and multiple review modes.

How can I contact support?

Use formycareersupport@gmail.com and we respond within 2-5 business days.
