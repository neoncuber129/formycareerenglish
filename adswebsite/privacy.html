Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FormyCareer (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our website (formycareer.vercel.app), the FormyCareer desktop application (Windows and macOS), and the FormyCareer mobile application. By using our services, you agree to this policy together with our Terms of Service.

1. Scope

This policy covers personal data we process as a controller through the properties above. Third-party sites linked from our website follow their own policies.

2. Data stored locally on your device

FormyCareer is built around your personal vocabulary library: words you save, SRS schedules, tags, audio preferences, and related study metadata are stored primarily in local databases on your device. We do not automatically upload your full vocabulary database to servers we operate unless you use optional features described below (such as signed-in sync).

3. Capture, OCR, screen access, and translation

Desktop versions may offer features that read text you select in other applications or capture text from a screen region you choose (including OCR). Processing may occur on your device and/or involve sending the selected text (or derived text) to translation or language services to provide meanings and audio. You should only capture content you are legally permitted to copy and process (including employer policies and third-party terms).

Where the operating system requires it (for example Accessibility or Screen Recording on macOS), permission prompts explain why access is requested. You can revoke permissions in system settings at any time.

4. Optional Google account and cloud sync

When available and only if you choose to connect a Google account, the apps may use OAuth to obtain tokens stored securely on your device and sync an encrypted snapshot with Google Drive application data for backup or multi-device continuity. If this feature is disabled or unavailable in your build, no Google OAuth flow runs. Disconnecting or uninstalling may leave residual tokens until cleared by you or the OS.

5. FormyCareer Pro and license verification

If you purchase or activate FormyCareer Pro, license keys or related identifiers may be sent over HTTPS to our payment or license verification providers (for example Lemon Squeezy or auxiliary verification endpoints we operate) to validate entitlement and prevent fraud. We process only what those transactions require.

6. Remote configuration (desktop)

The desktop app may periodically fetch a small JSON configuration file from our domain (for example update prompts, fair-use caps on free tiers, feature flags, or ad banner URLs). These requests include ordinary HTTP metadata (such as IP address and user-agent). They are not intended to collect your vocabulary contents.

7. Website usage, cookies, and ads

On this website we may use essential cookies for security and consent. With your consent, advertising or analytics cookies may be used. See our Cookie Policy.

We may use third-party advertising services (such as Google AdSense or similar) that use cookies or identifiers to deliver and measure ads. Those providers process data under their own policies. We do not sell your personal information.

8. How we use information (summary)

  • Operate and secure FormyCareer on web and apps.
  • Provide translations, audio, sync, licensing, and updates.
  • Improve reliability and diagnose technical issues.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms.

9. Sharing and processors

We do not sell personal data. We share information only with service providers strictly necessary to deliver features you use (hosting, payments, licensing, translation/TTS infrastructure, analytics or ads where enabled). Providers are bound by confidentiality and processing obligations appropriate to their role.

10. Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including legal, accounting, or security requirements. Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app (subject to OS backups).

11. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No online or offline system is perfectly secure; use strong device protections and keep your apps updated.

12. International transfers

Our infrastructure and subprocessors may be located outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) or statutory exceptions.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, rectify, delete, restrict, or port certain personal data, or to object to processing. Contact us to exercise these rights. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

For account-enabled builds, you can submit an account deletion request at /account-deletion.html.

14. Children

FormyCareer is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it promptly where required by law.

15. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, additional notice in the app or on the website.

16. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: formycareersupport@gmail.com.
