Inside FormyCareer: Capture, Review, and Retain Vocabulary

Published May 1, 2026

FormyCareer is built for one goal: help you remember useful vocabulary from real work and interview contexts. This guide shows the core workflow using three key screens in the app.

1. Capture words from real context

Instead of memorizing disconnected lists, start by capturing words from articles, documents, job descriptions, and materials you already read. This keeps learning directly tied to your goals.

  • Add term and meaning quickly from selected text.
  • Save examples from real sentences to retain usage.
  • Tag by topic such as interview, product, or analytics.

2. Review with SRS for long-term retention

FormyCareer uses spaced repetition to schedule what you should review next. You spend time on cards that need reinforcement instead of reviewing everything repeatedly.

  • Run short daily sessions to keep momentum.
  • Use graded responses to adjust review intervals.
  • Focus on difficult items until recall becomes automatic.

3. Organize your vocabulary library

A clean library makes review faster. Use search, tags, and archive controls to keep active study material relevant to your current role and interview targets.

  • Filter by tag to study one context at a time.
  • Archive low-priority cards without deleting history.
  • Keep active cards aligned with your current goals.

Simple daily workflow

Capture during the day, review in one focused session, then clean up your library weekly. This lightweight loop helps vocabulary stick and makes learning sustainable even with a busy schedule.
