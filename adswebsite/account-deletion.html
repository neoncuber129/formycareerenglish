Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: May 2, 2026

If your build of FormyCareer uses account features, you can request account and related cloud data deletion by emailing formycareersupport@gmail.com.

What to include

  • Email address used to sign in
  • Device platform (Android/iOS/Desktop)
  • Subject line: Account deletion request

What will be deleted

  • Account profile and authentication mapping (if present)
  • Cloud backup/sync metadata managed by FormyCareer services

What may remain

  • Financial records kept for legal compliance
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics already processed

We process valid deletion requests within 30 days.
