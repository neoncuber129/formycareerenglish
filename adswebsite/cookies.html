Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

This policy explains how FormyCareer uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

1. What cookies are used

  • Essential cookies: required for security, page behavior, and consent preferences.
  • Preference cookies: used to remember choices (for example, cookie consent decisions).
  • Advertising/analytics cookies: may be set by third-party services when enabled.

2. Why we use cookies

  • Keep the site secure and functional.
  • Remember privacy and consent settings.
  • Measure service performance and ad effectiveness when configured.

3. Third-party services

Depending on configuration, this site may use services from Google (including AdSense/AdMob), GitHub links, or other providers. These providers may set their own cookies according to their policies.

4. Managing your choices

You can manage consent from the cookie banner and through browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect certain features.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to formycareersupport@gmail.com.
