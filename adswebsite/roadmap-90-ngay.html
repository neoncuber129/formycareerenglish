90-Day Roadmap to Transition Your Career

Published May 1, 2026

Key takeaways

  • Choose one concrete target role before selecting courses or projects.
  • Produce weekly proof of skill, not just notes.
  • Use feedback from interviews to shape the next learning cycle.

A career transition does not happen overnight. A 90-day plan helps you stay focused, avoid random learning, and build proof of progress before applying.

Phase 1 (Days 1-30): Assess and Define Target Role

Start by listing existing experience, transferable skills, and your reason for switching. Pick one specific target role, such as "Junior Data Analyst" instead of broad goals. Review 20-30 job descriptions to identify recurring requirements.

Phase 2 (Days 31-60): Build Core Skills and Portfolio Proof

Focus on 2-3 core skills that appear most often in job postings. Pair every skill with practical exercises. At the end of each week, complete one mini project that demonstrates your ability to apply what you learned.

Phase 3 (Days 61-90): Finalize Profile and Apply Strategically

Update your CV with measurable outcomes, optimize LinkedIn, and apply to roles that match your strengths. After each interview, document difficult questions and convert them into your next practice session.

Weekly Checklist

  • 5-7 focused learning hours on core skills.
  • 1 tangible output (project, article, or short report).
  • 1 weekly review to refine your roadmap.
  • 2-3 high-quality applications.

Consistency matters more than intensity. In 90 days, you can create a visible career shift with clear tracking and regular improvement.
